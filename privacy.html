Privacy policy

This policy describes the private Workspace Utility integration and this informational website.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on permissions granted, Workspace Utility accesses Gmail messages and metadata, calendar events, contacts, and Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets content to perform account-owner-authorized workflows. Granted permissions can also support changes and sending messages; access permissions alone do not authorize every action.

Processing and providers

Account data may be processed on the operator’s hosted system and by configured AI providers to carry out requested workflows. Selected summaries may be delivered through configured messaging services. Those providers may process and retain information under their applicable terms and settings. This public website contains no Google account data.

Storage and retention

The integration stores authorization credentials and may retain retrieved content, summaries, conversation history, logs, and backups for ongoing workflows and recovery. Retention varies by record type, operational need, and provider; there is no single automatic deletion period for all records.

Revocation and deletion

The account owner can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access using that authorization but does not automatically delete previously retained copies. Contact the operator below to request deletion of retained records. Deletion is handled separately for active storage, backups, and service providers, subject to applicable technical and legal retention requirements.

Website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or sign-in forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may collect ordinary request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
